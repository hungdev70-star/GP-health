# GP Health language targets

The product target is 20 Indian interface languages:

1. Hindi
2. Bengali
3. Telugu
4. Marathi
5. Tamil
6. Urdu
7. Gujarati
8. Kannada
9. Malayalam
10. Punjabi
11. Odia
12. Assamese
13. Maithili
14. Sanskrit
15. Kashmiri
16. Konkani
17. Manipuri (Meitei)
18. Bodo
19. Dogri
20. Santali

The app must always offer manual language selection. Automatic spoken-language detection is an additional convenience, not the only way to select a language.

Important: a language being listed as a UI target does not mean offline speech recognition or text-to-speech is available on every Android or iOS device. Detect installed platform language support at runtime, explain unavailable packs clearly, and retain large-button/manual interaction as a fallback. Do not promise always-listening wake-word parity across iOS and Android.
